The core loop

Five steps, every time — nothing skipped.

Most advice skips straight to what to write. This works backward from what's actually happening in the thread first.

1

Diagnose

Read the conversation state and closing readiness from what was actually said — not a template.

2

Decide

Weigh the observed signals against the risks to land on one strategic objective.

3

Respond

Get response options built for that objective — plus the one message you shouldn't send.

4

Observe

Know exactly what to watch for in their reply, and what it actually means.

5

Adapt

Silence gets diagnosed too — wait, reopen, change angle, or stop. Never "just checking in."

The other half of the answer

What you shouldn't send, and why it's tempting.

Every recommendation comes with its opposite: the message that feels safe to send but quietly kills momentum. Knowing why it's wrong — for this exact conversation — is what actually changes how you write the next one.

✕ Don't send

"Totally understand! We've helped dozens of companies migrate without any issues. Want to jump on a quick 15-min call?"

Answers a fear they haven't named yet, and asks for more commitment than the state can currently support.

✓ Send this instead

"Do you know if your custom fields are mostly standard property types, or did someone build a bunch of complex dependencies?"

Stays inside the diagnosis — surfaces the real migration risk without asking them to commit to anything yet.
